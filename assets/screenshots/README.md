## Provider billing references

[Morning](./usage/providerUsageMorning.png), [afternoon](./usage/providerUsageAfternoon.png), [late afternoon](./usage/providerUsageLateAfternoon.png), and [evening](./usage/providerUsageEvening.png) are external provider dashboard captures. Their date/API-key filters and hourly totals are not Session-scoped measurements. They are not used to claim a T2 price, an application-wide cache rate or a product comparison.

The PNG files remain byte-for-byte copies of the supplied originals. Review local usernames, paths, hostnames and account labels before public distribution. Do not publish credentials.

The supplied Skill example shows loading its instructions, not completing a task using that Skill. The multi-image screenshots show history layout, not model understanding of every image.
